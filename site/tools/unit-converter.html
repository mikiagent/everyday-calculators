Metric and imperial units

Almost every country uses the metric system, built on the metre, kilogram and litre with units that scale by powers of ten. The United States still uses customary units such as inches, pounds and gallons for everyday measurement, and the United Kingdom mixes both, using miles for roads and pints for beer alongside metric elsewhere.

Handy conversions to remember

  • 1 inch is exactly 2.54 cm, and 1 mile is about 1.6 km.
  • 1 kilogram is about 2.2 pounds; 1 pound is about 454 grams.
  • 1 US gallon is about 3.8 litres, but 1 UK gallon is about 4.5 litres, so recipes and fuel figures differ between the two.
  • To convert Celsius to Fahrenheit, double it and add 30 for a rough answer, or multiply by 1.8 and add 32 for the exact one.
