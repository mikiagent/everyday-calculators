How percentages work

A percentage is a fraction out of one hundred. "15% of 200" means 15 hundredths of 200, so you multiply 200 by 0.15 to get 30. To find what percent one number is of another, divide the part by the whole and multiply by 100.

Percentage change

Percentage change compares a new value with an original value: subtract the original from the new value, divide by the original, then multiply by 100. Going from 80 to 100 is a 25% increase, but going from 100 back to 80 is only a 20% decrease, because the base you divide by is different.

Common uses

  • Working out a discount in a sale: a 30% discount on $60 saves $18, so you pay $42.
  • Adding sales tax or VAT to a price.
  • Comparing growth between two periods, such as monthly revenue or website visits.
  • Grading: 45 correct out of 60 questions is 75%.
