Counting days correctly

Whether to include the end day depends on what you are counting. For a hotel stay from the 1st to the 4th you pay for three nights, so you do not include the end day. For a rental or a project that runs from the 1st to the 4th inclusive, you count four days. Tick "include end day" when both ends belong to the period.

Common uses

  • Counting down to a holiday, wedding or deadline.
  • Working out how many days remain on a notice period or warranty.
  • Finding the exact age of a person, pet or project in years, months and days.
  • Calculating a due date by adding a fixed number of days to an invoice date.

All calculations use calendar dates, so daylight-saving changes never add or remove a day from the result.
