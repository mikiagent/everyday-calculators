How much should you tip?

In the United States, 15% to 20% of the pre-tax bill is the usual range for table service, with 18% to 20% now common in cities. Counter service and takeout are often tipped less, or not at all. In many other countries service is included and tipping is a small gesture rather than an expectation, so check local custom when travelling.

Tips for splitting the bill

  • Decide the tip on the whole bill first, then divide, so everyone contributes equally to service.
  • Round each share up to a convenient amount; the few cents of difference go to the server.
  • If one person ordered far more, split by items instead and add the same percentage to each share.
