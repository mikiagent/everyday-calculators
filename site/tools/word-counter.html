Why word counts matter

Many formats have length limits or expectations. A tweet allows 280 characters, a meta description should stay under about 160, college essays often cap at 650 words, and a typical blog post runs 1,000 to 2,000 words. Knowing the count as you write avoids a painful trim at the end.

How the estimates work

Reading time assumes about 238 words per minute, the average for adults reading non-fiction silently. Speaking time uses 150 words per minute, a comfortable pace for presentations. Sentences are counted by terminal punctuation, so abbreviations like "e.g." can inflate the number slightly.

Nothing you type here leaves your browser. The counting happens on your device and the text is not stored or sent anywhere.
