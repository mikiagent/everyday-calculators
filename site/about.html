About Everyday Calculators

Everyday Calculators is a small collection of free tools for the arithmetic people actually need day to day: splitting a bill, checking a loan payment, converting a recipe, counting days to a deadline.

Every calculator runs entirely in your browser. Nothing you enter is sent to a server or stored. The site is supported by advertising, which is what keeps it free and lets us keep it online.

Accuracy

Each tool explains the formula it uses. We test the calculators against known values, but they are provided for general information and should not replace advice from a qualified professional for financial, medical or legal decisions.
